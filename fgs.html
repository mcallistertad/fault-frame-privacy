Fault Frame connected-device service demonstration

For Google Play review: a user starts a diagnostic recording, sees the ongoing Android notification, returns to the recording and disconnects. No account or external hardware is needed to play this video.

This demonstration uses Fault Frame's built-in simulator on an Android 16 emulator. All diagnostic readings shown are synthetic.

Unedited screen recording, 2 minutes 33 seconds, no audio. The sequence is described below. Open the video directly (MP4, 10.2 MB).

What the video shows

  1. 0:05 — App details. About Fault Frame shows version 0.1.0 (1) and the release build.
  2. 0:25 — Data source. The overview identifies the built-in simulator and simulated adapter/protocol.
  3. 0:45 — Recording. A drive log is recording while the selected live-data graphs update.
  4. 0:50–1:00 — Ongoing notification. Android's notification shade shows “Drive log recording · live data running”, with Stop drive log, Stop live + save log and Disconnect actions.
  5. 1:05 — Return to the app. The graph remains active and the recorded reading count has increased.
  6. 1:35–2:00 — Stop and save. Recording stops; the saved simulator log shows Complete, 34 seconds, 1,893 readings and 36 of 36 supported parameters sampled.
  7. 2:15–2:30 — Disconnect. The notification's Disconnect action is used, and the Fault Frame notification disappears.

Review context

Recorded on 10 September 2026 from an earlier production AAB-derived build of version 0.1.0 (1), locally signed with a development certificate for emulator execution. This recording predates the upload key created on 11 September and does not show the exact signed bundle uploaded to Google Play.

The demonstration shows the simulator workflow and notification controls. It does not establish real-adapter compatibility, vehicle behaviour, sustained performance, locked-screen operation or Doze reliability. The original native H.264 video is provided without editing or re-encoding.

In the app, choose Scan → Use simulator to review the diagnostic features without a vehicle. The connected-device service supports the user's selected adapter session during diagnostic scans, live data and recording; interruption can create gaps or interrupt an active diagnostic operation.
