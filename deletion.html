Fault Frame: delete your data

Fault Frame is published by Thaddeus McAllister in Ireland under the developer name voxrelay. There is no Fault Frame account to delete. You can request deletion of information you have sent to the publisher and remove records stored on your device.

Request deletion by email

  1. Email voxrelaynew@gmail.com with the subject Fault Frame data deletion.
  2. Say which support messages or attachments you want deleted. If known, include the subject or approximate date of your earlier message so it can be found.
  3. Send the request from the email address used for that correspondence where possible. You do not need to reinstall the app or sign in to request deletion.

You can use the same address for help with deletion. Do not include a full VIN or new diagnostic attachments in a routine request.

Information held by the publisher

This request covers support and feedback correspondence held by the publisher: your email address, any name supplied by your email, message contents, attachments you chose to send and normal email headers. The app does not automatically upload saved scans, drive logs or Garage entries to the publisher.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to answer the request, investigate and resolve the reported issue, and deal with a related follow-up or dispute. The criteria are whether the matter is resolved, whether the information is still needed for that matter, and whether a specific legal obligation requires it to be kept. Information that is no longer needed for these purposes is to be deleted. Information needed to fulfil a data-protection request or another specific legal duty is processed to meet that obligation.

Delete records stored on your device

The publisher cannot access or remotely delete records held only on your device. You control these records:

  • Saved scans and drive logs: choose Delete saved scan in Saved scans, or Delete from phone in Saved drive logs, then confirm the deletion. These records remain until you delete them, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Garage entries: choose Delete from garage for the vehicle in Settings → Garage, then confirm, to remove its name, notes and saved adapter choice. Deleting a Garage entry does not delete saved drive logs; delete those separately.
  • Preferences and all app-private records: use Android Settings to open Fault Frame's app information and clear its storage, or uninstall the app. Android's exact menu names vary by phone. Clearing storage removes saved scans, drive logs, Garage entries and preferences.
  • Session VIN and vehicle identity: these are cleared when you disconnect or the diagnostic session is reset. They are not automatically added to Garage, saved scans, drive logs or diagnostic reports.

Exports, temporary copies and external services

Clearing Fault Frame's storage or uninstalling it does not remove reports or recordings already exported elsewhere. Delete those files in the destination you selected, or ask the receiving app or person to remove their copies.

Temporary files used for sharing are eligible for the app's bounded cleanup after at least 24 hours. They are not guaranteed to disappear exactly at that time, and Android may clear cache storage independently.

If you consented to a VIN lookup, NHTSA received the VIN and normal connection information such as your IP address. Disable automatic lookup in Settings to prevent future automatic requests. A completed request cannot be retracted through Fault Frame. NHTSA controls its own storage and deletion; its privacy policy says automatically collected website-visit information is retained indefinitely.

The support mailbox uses Gmail. Deleting correspondence from the publisher's mailbox does not guarantee deletion of every copy held by your mail provider, Google or another recipient. Google describes its handling and retention in its privacy policy. GitHub hosts this page and controls its service logs under its privacy statement.

For full data-handling details and your privacy rights, read the Fault Frame privacy policy.
